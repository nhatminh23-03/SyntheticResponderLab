# Cortado Roasters — Everyday Origins Subscription Survey

## Survey Setup
- **Target population:** US adults who drink coffee at home at least a few times per week.
- **Mode:** Online self-administered survey.
- **Estimated LOI:** 6–7 minutes
- **Primary output:** Demand, price sensitivity, barriers, and positioning for a direct-to-consumer coffee subscription.
- **Questions:** ~24 items

## Section C: Product Stimulus

**[Respondents read the following product description before answering.]**

**Product Description — Everyday Origins by Cortado Roasters**

Everyday Origins is a direct-to-consumer coffee subscription from Cortado Roasters. Every bag is roasted to order and shipped within 24 hours of roasting, so it arrives days rather than months after roast date.

Subscribers choose a roast profile (light, medium, or dark), a grind (whole bean, drip, espresso, or French press), and a delivery cadence (every 1, 2, or 4 weeks). The plan starts at $19 per 12 oz bag with free shipping, and subscribers can skip, pause, swap the roast, or cancel at any time from their account with no fee.

Beans are single-origin and traceable to the cooperative that grew them, with the farm, elevation, and harvest window printed on every bag. Cortado Roasters pays a published premium above the Fair Trade floor price and reports the exact figure paid to producers each quarter.
